About the disease

Parkinson's disease

A progressive neurological disorder that affects movement. Common motor signs include tremor, stiffness, and slowed motion.

Handwriting and spiral drawing can show irregular velocity and jerk, the kind of kinematic signal this demo explores.

Passion research project for learning. Not clinical advice or a diagnostic tool.
Dataset: UCI spiral drawings
Model: XGBoost + kinematics
